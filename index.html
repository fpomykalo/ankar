The R&D challenge

The R&D challenge

Supporting evidence

Innovation has
outgrown the
systems behind it.

R&D teams have more information than ever. But the knowledge behind the next breakthrough is scattered across experiments, technical documents, scientific literature, patents, specialist systems and the people who carry critical context in their heads.

Breakthroughs often happen between disciplines. Yet teams still spend enormous amounts of time finding information, reconciling different sources and recreating work that has already been done. Legacy tools can store individual pieces of the puzzle, but they were not built to connect the full context—or help teams act on it.

The cost is bigger than inefficiency. It is slower iteration, repeated work, knowledge that disappears between projects and the risk of missing the technology that could define what an organization becomes next.

Insight

In a more competitive world, innovation is the difference
between leading what comes next and being left behind.
