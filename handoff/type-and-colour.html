Cap-height trimming. Headings, labels and button text are set with text-box-trim: trim-both; text-box-edge: cap alphabetic. The line box is cut to the cap height, so a value like "title top at 20px" means the top of the capitals sits 20px below the rule. Without this property the same text renders about 3px lower on 12px labels and more on large headings.

Smoothing. Body text uses -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased, which is what makes the 200-weight display face read as light as it does on macOS.

Gradients

The large colour fields are photographs of gradients, not CSS gradients. Constants worth knowing

Shape and rhythm

  • Container 1280px inside 80px gutters on the 1440px artboard. Sections are 120px apart, measured from the previous content to the next divider.
  • Corner radius 30px on every large panel, card and glass bar; 20px on buttons and tabs; 10px on the mega-menu cards.
  • Eyebrow dot 8px, 10px gap to its label. Section head: divider, eyebrow 20px below, title 58px below.
  • Folder cards are 480px tall with a 25px tab on the left; labels read bottom to top inside the tab.
  • Easing: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) for arrivals, cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1) for in-out moves.
