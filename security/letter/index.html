By Wiem and Tam

Co-founders at Ankar

Security from Day One

From day one at Ankar, we made a defining choice: security would come first - not later. Before we signed our first customer, we had already achieved the industry’s highest security certifications. That conviction comes from experience.

Before founding Ankar, we [Wiem and Tamar] spent years at Palantir, building mission-critical systems that safeguarded some of the world’s most sensitive national-security data. Wiem led secure, enterprise-scale AI deployments and helped develop new AI technologies. Tamar Gomez later spent time leading products at Helsing, a European defense-technology company developing secure AI capabilities for governments and military organizations across Europe.

With over a decade of experience building defense-grade software, the Ankar platform was designed with security at its core. We apply the same rigor once used to safeguard classified defense information to the world of R&D and intellectual property.

Security Is Not a Department.
It’s Our Culture

At Ankar, security is a mindset that is embedded in every decision. Our engineers, product leaders, and customer teams share a single responsibility: protecting our customers’ data.

We come from environments where failure to protect information was never an option - and that discipline still guides how we design, test, and operate today.

Both co-founders, our Head of Engineering, and our Head of Product are former Palantir engineers with hands-on experience building and securing defense-grade systems. Several team members are ex-military, bringing with them a culture of precision, accountability, and operational security.

This collective background ensures that security isn’t a static function - it’s an organizational reflex. Every line of code, every deployment, and every integration is reviewed through a security lens before it reaches production.

No Data Sharing.
No Model Training.
No Exceptions

Ankar never uses your data to train models. Your information is never shared.

We understand that the material we handle (R&D ideas, invention disclosures, and patent drafts) must never enter the public domain. That’s why we built Ankar around a principle we call data sovereignty by design: your data stays exactly where it belongs - in your hands.

All customer data is securely stored within our Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) environment, fully segregated and encrypted both in transit and at rest. We deploy local copies of large-language models within secure cloud environments, ensuring that data in the Ankar platform never leaves these isolated environments, and will never be accessible to model providers.

Ankar is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. These are two of the most rigorous, globally recognized standards for data security and confidentiality. Achieving these certifications before onboarding our first customer reflects a commitment to security that has been foundational since day one.

Built Like the Systems
That Protect Nations

Our platform is architected with the same rigor we once applied to defense-grade environments:

  • End-to-end encryption for all data in transit and at rest
  • Fully segregated customer environments within a secure multi-tenant infrastructure
  • Strict role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication (MFA)
  • Comprehensive audit trails ensuring full accountability
  • Flexible data-residency options in both the US and EU

These aren’t just security controls - they are design principles that make unauthorized access not merely unlikely, but structurally impossible.

Our guiding philosophy is simplicity and predictability: reduce complexity, eliminate entire classes of vulnerabilities, and make abnormal behavior instantly detectable. This clarity of design is what enables Ankar to deliver both speed and safety at enterprise scale.

Trusted by the World’s
Most Secure Enterprises

Today, Ankar has earned the confidence of CISOs worldwide, powering Fortune 500 companies and leading global law firms as they manage some of the world’s most valuable IP portfolios.

Our customers include several of Europe’s leading dual use firms, who rely on Ankar to manage their most sensitive R&D and IP data. If organizations trusted to protect nations trust us, you can trust us too.

Security has been in our DNA from day one, and our track record reflects it: we’ve built a platform that satisfies enterprise-grade security standards without ever slowing innovation.

Security Is Our Foundation

At Ankar, security isn’t a product feature. It’s our foundation. We continuously invest in infrastructure, testing, and governance.

Protecting customer data isn’t a milestone or a marketing claim, it’s a daily discipline. The systems we build today carry the same responsibility as those we once built to protect nations.

Because for us, trust isn’t claimed. It’s engineered.
